Our Story

1
School Days & A 12-Year Pause

Crossing Paths Again

Harsh and Sneha knew each other way back from school — but had zero contact for twelve whole years. Cut to Covid: they reconnected through a mutual friend at a workout place and started carpooling together.

2
The Everyday Comfort

Natural & Unpretentious

They met, spoke for hours, and somewhere along the way found something rather rare — the comfort of simply being themselves.

3
Little Surprises & Deep Roots

Quiet Understanding

From the little surprises Harsh gave Sneha — Harry Potter treasures tucked into her room, flowers on special occasions — to the appreciation, comfort, and patient listening Sneha gave him in return, and the sense of stability, simplicity, and laughter she brought to his life — it was never about grand declarations. Just two people who found companionship in the everyday, and a quiet understanding that felt natural from the beginning.

4
Two Worlds Collide

Architect & Oncologist

He's an architect, passionate about his work and sports. She's a radiation oncologist, passionate about dance. Their love for reality shows, good food dates, and simple chores brought them even closer — two very different worlds, yet remarkably similar in their ambition, values, and deep love for family and traditions.

5
Long-Distance Love

#OneFirstAtATime

For three years, they mastered the art of long-distance love — living in different rectangles on a screen, planning their monthly meets like important calendar appointments, and making sure every anniversary came with a new place, a new adventure, and another story to take home. So many firsts, for the both of them. #OneFirstAtATime ❤️

💍
The Next Chapter

Building a Life Together

What began with long conversations slowly became a life built on comfort, companionship, respect, and laughter. And somewhere between designing, healing, dancing, travelling, and cheering from the sidelines, they decided that perhaps the best thing to build together was a life.

Two passions. Two families. One beautiful journey. ❤️
